# Software engineered in Botswana.

**Innovateium** (i·noh·vayt·ium) is a software engineering company in Gaborone, Botswana. Since 2021 we have designed, built, and maintained digital systems for enterprises, financial institutions, and growing businesses, and we are now building products of our own.

We build the systems, stay for the maintenance, and answer the phone.

## What we build

- **Enterprise systems.** Registration, records, and public-facing platforms at national scale, including perfective maintenance of live systems that thousands of people rely on every day.
- **Custom software and platforms.** Web, mobile, and desktop systems designed around how an organisation actually works.
- **Payments and fintech integration.** Secure, compliant payment flows built in from the start.
- **Process automation and systems integration.** Replacing manual back-office work, and connecting new products to the APIs and platforms you already run.
- **Support and maintenance.** We troubleshoot problems through to resolution, long after launch day.
